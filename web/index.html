Indian Cities Lose 30-50% Treated Water to Hidden Leaks

Most Indian urban centres (Delhi, Bengaluru, Shimla) receive water for only 2 to 4 hours per day (Intermittent Water Supply — IWS).

All standard commercial leak detection systems assume continuous, 24/7 pressurized flow with smooth diurnal profiles. Under IWS, pipes empty and refill every day, causing extreme transients and masking leaks.

Why Continuous Models Fail

Pipes fill with air between supply cycles. When water is pumped, air pockets cause massive surge waves, followed by brief supply windows with volatile consumer drawing.

Figure 1: Demand Swings Mask Leak Anomalies

Day-to-day consumer demand naturally swings by ±10%. In an intermittent pipe network, this ±10% swing moves junction pressure head by 0.8 to 1.5 metres.

A medium pipe leak (7.5% of total supply) also causes a pressure drop of ~1.2 metres. Therefore, a naive fixed pressure threshold confuses normal demand surges with leaks, catching only 49% of leaks at 5% False Alarm Rate.

Key Insight

Plain Threshold: 49% detection rate.
Demand Masking Problem: A hot day with +8% water demand looks mathematically identical to an active pipe rupture!

ΔP_demand ≈ -7.2 × (g - 1.0) ≈ ±0.9 m head
ΔP_leak ≈ -1.1 m head (Confounded!)
Mathematical Innovation: Demand Cancellation Residual

Because consumer demand affects all sensors simultaneously via network-wide sensitivity vectors s, we project out the common mode:

ˆg = (s • r) / (s • s)
r_c = r - s × ˆg
d = ||r_c||

Multi-Cycle Persistence Rule: Alarm only fires if d > 1.10 in 2 of the last 3 supply cycles.

Honest Limits & Engineering Scope

What Boond Solves

  • Eliminates demand masking false alarms under intermittent supply.
  • Catches 78% of medium/large bursts within 1-2 supply cycles.
  • Localises leak origin to neighbourhood sector (top-5 candidates).
  • Works with modest sensor density (10-20 per DMA).

Honest Limitations

  • Simulation-based: Validated on EPANET WNTR models; requires field pilot validation.
  • Small leaks (<2.5%): Hard to distinguish from sensor drift under low sensor counts (5 sensors).
  • Neighbourhood-level: Narrow down to 2-3 pipe blocks; final pinpointing requires acoustic listening sticks.
