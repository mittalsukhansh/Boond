Indian Cities Lose 30-50% Treated Water to Hidden Leaks

Most Indian urban centres (Delhi, Bengaluru, Shimla) receive water for only 2 to 4 hours per day (Intermittent Water Supply — IWS). Standard leak detection assumes continuous 24/7 pressurized flow. When pipes drain and refill every day, continuous models break down completely.

Figure 1: Demand Swings Mask Leak Signals

Consumer drawing varies by ±10% day to day. In an intermittent network, this ±10% swing moves junction pressure head by 0.8 to 1.5 metres—the same magnitude as a pipe rupture! Therefore, a plain fixed threshold catches only 49% of leaks.

Mathematical Innovation: Demand-Cancelled Residual

We project out the systemic demand swing, then require the signal to persist across supply cycles: raises detection from 49% to 78% at 20 sensors.
